# M1 — Product Reframing & Core Problem Definition

**State:** Stage 1 done — Product PRD pending human review · **Date:** 2026-05-20

Goal: reframe the product from "generic project generation" to a coach that
helps job-seeking junior devs understand, review, debug, and explain their
AI-assisted projects. M1 is a CCPM **Plan** milestone — definition, not code.

## Brainstorm decisions (2026-05-20)

- **Target user:** job-seeking junior devs (bootcamp grads & self-taught) who
  built AI-assisted projects and can't explain them in interviews.
- **Core pains:** can't explain my own project; don't understand the stack.
- **Positioning:** coaches existing repos; teaches understanding not generation;
  job-market artifacts; comprehension-checked; real-time visualization of the
  industrial development workflow.
- **Primary success metric:** user ships a portfolio project they fully
  understand and can defend.
